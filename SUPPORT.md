# Support

How to get help with INNOVATION SYNERGY AI software.

## If you are using one of our apps

Most of our apps are built for a specific business, and that business handles its own customers. Use the contact or call option inside the app first. If the app itself is broken rather than the service behind it, email **michael@innovationsynergyai.com** and include:

- The app name and version, shown at the bottom of the app's settings or contact screen
- Your device model and iOS version
- What you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead
- A screenshot or screen recording if the problem is visual

## If you are a client

Use the channel we agreed on for your engagement. Email **michael@innovationsynergyai.com** for anything urgent or anything that has no owner yet. Production-down issues should say so in the subject line.

## If you want to hire us

Email **michael@innovationsynergyai.com** with what you are trying to build, who it is for, and any deadline you are working against. Useful things to mention: whether you need iPhone only or iPad and Mac as well, what systems the app has to talk to, and whether an App Store listing already exists.

## If you found a bug in a public repository

Open an issue in that repository. Security vulnerabilities are the exception and should go through the process in `SECURITY.md` instead.

## What this is not

GitHub issues are not a support channel for end users of client apps, and we cannot look up your account, appointment, or quote from here.
