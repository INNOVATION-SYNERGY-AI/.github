# Code of Conduct

This applies to every public space this organization runs: issues, pull requests, discussions, and code review.

## The standard

Be straightforward and be decent about it. Direct technical criticism is welcome and expected. Criticize the code, the design, or the decision, not the person who made it.

Specifically, we expect people to:

- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith until there is real evidence otherwise
- Say what they mean plainly, and explain the reasoning behind a strong opinion
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on scope and direction, even when they disagree
- Keep client names, customer data, and anything under NDA out of public threads

And not to:

- Harass, insult, demean, or make personal attacks
- Post sexualized or otherwise inappropriate content
- Publish anyone's private information without their permission
- Derail threads with off-topic argument, spam, or self-promotion
- Ignore a maintainer's request to drop a line of discussion

## Scope

This covers public activity in this organization's repositories and any place where someone is representing the organization.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **michael@innovationsynergyai.com**. Reports stay confidential; we will not share your name with the person you are reporting without asking you first.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock a thread, or block an account. What we do depends on how bad it is and whether it keeps happening. A single clumsy comment usually earns a note asking you to adjust. Harassment earns a block.

Enforcement decisions are made by the maintainers of this organization and are final.
